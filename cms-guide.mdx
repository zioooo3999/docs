The content management system is Strapi 5, accessible at \[cms-url\]/admin. This section covers the common content operations for internal users. Screenshots are to be captured from the production instance.

## **_12.1 Login_**

Open \[cms-url\]/admin, enter the registered email and password, and click Login. \[Screenshot: login page\]

## **_12.2 Dashboard_**

After logging in, the Content Manager lists all collection types and single types in the left sidebar. \[Screenshot: dashboard / content manager\]

## **_12.3 Managing Banners_**

Banners and hero content are managed through the relevant page single type (for example the Home page) using its dynamic-zone components. Open the page, edit the banner component fields and images, then Save and Publish. \[Screenshot: banner component\]

## **_12.4 Managing Articles / News_**

In Content Manager, open the News collection. Use Create new entry to add an article, fill in the title, slug, content and cover image, assign a category, then Save and Publish. \[Screenshot: news entry\]

## **_12.5 Managing Gallery_**

Gallery images are managed through the media components on the relevant page, and through the Media Library for the underlying files. \[Screenshot: gallery / media component\]

## **_12.6 Managing Pages_**

Open the Pages collection to edit page content and its dynamic-zone components (sections). Each page is identified by its slug (see Sitemap). Save and Publish to apply changes. \[Screenshot: page editor\]

## **_12.7 Uploading Media_**

Use the Media Library (or the media field within an entry) to upload images and files. Uploaded files are stored in the MinIO bucket. \[Screenshot: media library upload\]

## **_12.8 Publishing Content_**

Click Publish on an entry to make it visible on the frontend. Published entries are served through the public API.

## **_12.9 Draft Content_**

Save without publishing to keep an entry as a draft. Drafts are not shown on the public frontend.

## **_12.10 Deleting Content_**

Open the entry and choose Delete, or select multiple entries in the list view and delete them together. \[Screenshot: delete confirmation\]

## **_12.11 Search & Filter Content_**

Use the search box and column filters in each collection list view to locate entries by title, status, or other fields.

## **_12.12 Logout_**

Open the user menu at the bottom-left of the admin panel and choose Logout.
