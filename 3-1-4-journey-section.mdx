## Overview

- Featuring a section headline “Discover Experiences That Inspire Curiosity” as an introduction to exploring activities at Tempe Park.
- Presenting content in the form of a visual card grid layout consisting of several highlights of key places or experiences.
- Each card contains a representative image, a short title, and a “View This Place” CTA to direct users to further details.
- Use a variety of card sizes (large & small) to create visual hierarchy and draw attention to the main content.
- Equipped with a gradient overlay on the card to maintain the readability of the text above the image.
- It has navigation categories at the bottom such as “Attractions”, “Activities”, and “Facilities” to make it easier to explore the experience page.
- Functions as an entry point for users to understand the various experiences available at Tempe Park visually and interactively.

## User Story

<Accordion title="View User Story" defaultOpen>
  - **As a user**, I want to visually see the various experiences and activities available at Tempe Park to encourage me to explore further.
  - I like to get a quick overview of each place or activity before diving into the details.
  - I want to be able to choose experience categories such as attractions, activities, or facilities according to my needs.
  - So I can plan my visit more easily and know what activities I want to do in Tempe Park.
</Accordion>
