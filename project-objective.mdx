## Strategic Approach

To address existing portal limitations, the website revamp project adopts a **user-journey-centric** and **experiential branding** approach. The platform is structured to guide visitors seamlessly from initial discovery to active park engagement and booking.

---

## Key Development Objectives

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Digital Gateway & Immersive Impression" color="#10b981" icon="compass">
    Deliver a modern, clean, and nature-inspired front-end interface that immediately communicates Tempe Park's core values, rich heritage, and recreational offerings.
  </Card>

  <Card title="User-Journey Information Architecture" color="#38bdf8" icon="sitemap">
    Restructure the site map into modular sections—such as **Plan Your Visit**, **Experience**, **Educations**, and **Innovation**—to guide users naturally from awareness to action.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Optimized Business Conversion" color="#f59e0b" icon="chart-line-up">
    Incorporate strategically placed CTAs for ticket purchases (_Regular & Group Tickets_), activity schedules, and program inquiries to streamline conversion flows.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Interactive Visiting Utilities" color="#6366f1" icon="map-location-dot">
    Provide intuitive tools—including an interactive area map, weekly schedule calendar, accessibility guides, and an innovation product catalog—for efficient trip planning.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
