## Overview

The Tempe Park frontend renders pages dynamically from Strapi using Next.js catch-all routes (`[[...slug]]`), meaning the page inventory is directly driven by the CMS content structure.

The primary navigation covers key site sections including **Home**, **Plan Your Visit**, **Experience**, **Education**, **Innovation**, **Production**, **News**, **Career**, and **Contact**.
