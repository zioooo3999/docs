## Overview

Tempe Park was established as an Edu-Park and tempeh innovation hub, aiming to serve as a primary center for education, inspiration, and experiential recreation centered around tempeh and its broader ecosystem.

To support this vision and strengthen its brand positioning, Tempe Park requires a centralized digital platform in the form of an official website to deliver comprehensive information, drive user engagement, and solidify its identity as a modern educational destination.

---

## Current Challenges & Problem Statement

An evaluation of the previous web platform revealed several key limitations that hindered brand storytelling and business conversion:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Conventional Visual Identity" color="#ef4444" icon="palette">
    Static corporate profile featuring dense text blocks, low-contrast color palettes, and visuals that failed to capture the physical park's immersive experience.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Suboptimal Navigation & UX" color="#f59e0b" icon="sitemap">
    Content architecture was not structured around the user journey, resulting in linear navigation and long scrolling without clear exploration cues.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Low Conversion & Hidden CTAs" color="#38bdf8" icon="bullseye">
    Inconsistent and underpromoted Calls-to-Action made it difficult for visitors to purchase tickets, register for educational programs, or submit group inquiries.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lack of Experiential Positioning" color="#10b981" icon="map-location-dot">
    Focused strictly on institutional information, failing to position Tempe Park as an exciting, family-friendly destination for recreation and learning.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Project Objectives & Strategic Solution

To address these limitations, the website revamp project adopts a user-journey-centric and experiential branding approach across four primary pillars:

| Objective Pillar | Strategic Implementation |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Digital Gateway & Immersive Design** | Deliver a modern, clean, and nature-inspired front-end interface that immediately communicates core values, heritage, and recreational offerings. |
| **User-Journey Information Architecture** | Restructure the sitemap into clear, modular sections (_Plan Your Visit_, _Experience_, _Educations_, _Innovation_) to guide users from awareness to action. |
| **Optimized Business Conversion** | Incorporate strategically placed CTAs for ticket purchases (Regular & Group), activity schedules, and program inquiries to streamline conversion funnels. |
| **Interactive Visiting Utilities** | Provide intuitive tools including an interactive area map, weekly schedule calendar, transportation/accessibility guides, and an innovation product catalog. |
