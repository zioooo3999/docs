## Overview

The **Experience Highlights** section serves as a primary visual entry point for users to discover and interact with the offerings at Tempe Park. It is designed to inspire curiosity through a structured, dynamic layout.

- **Header:** Features the headline, "Discover Experiences That Inspire Curiosity."
- **Grid Layout:** Utilizes a visual card grid with varying sizes (large and small) to establish a clear visual hierarchy.
- **Card Components:** Each card includes a representative image, a concise title, and a "View This Place" CTA.
- **Readability:** Cards are equipped with a gradient overlay to ensure text remains legible against imagery.
- **Navigation:** Includes category tabs at the bottom ("Attractions", "Activities", "Facilities") that allow users to filter the content view dynamically.

---

## User Story

- **Exploration:** As a user, I want to visually see various experiences and activities available at Tempe Park to encourage me to explore further.
- **Overview:** I want a quick overview of each place or activity before diving into the specific details.
- **Categorization:** I want to filter experience categories (attractions, activities, or facilities) based on my personal interests.
- **Planning:** Providing this information helps me plan my visit more easily and determine which activities I want to prioritize at Tempe Park.

  Ensure that all imagery used in the grid is high-resolution to maintain the aesthetic quality of the card components. The gradient overlays should be consistently applied to support accessibility standards across all categories.
